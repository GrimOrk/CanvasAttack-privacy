## Analytics

Our game uses Unity Analytics, a service provided by Unity Technologies, to understand gameplay, identify balancing issues, and improve the player experience.

With your permission, we collect gameplay statistics such as game sessions, completed levels, wins, losses, and interactions with game mechanics.

Unity Analytics may also process technical information, including IP addresses, device information, and installation-specific identifiers.

We do not intentionally include names, email addresses, Steam IDs, or other directly identifying information in our custom gameplay events.

**Analytics collection is optional and based on your consent.** You can disable it at any time in the game's settings without affecting gameplay.

## Data Processing

Analytics data is processed by Unity Technologies on our behalf and may be transferred and stored internationally. Unity Analytics retains personal data for up to 13 months by default.

For more information, please see [Unity's Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy).

## Your Choices and Rights
